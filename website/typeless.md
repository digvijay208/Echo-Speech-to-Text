## Dictate

### Removes filler words

Automatically removes filler words like "um," "uh," and "you know," making your transcriptions clear and professional.

### Removes repetition

Automatically detects and removes unnecessary and repeated words in your speech, ensuring your language is concise and easy to understand.

### Auto-edits when you change your mind

Typeless recognizes when you correct yourself mid-sentence and keeps only your final intended message, avoiding clutter and confusion.

### Auto-formats

Automatically organizes spoken lists, steps, and key points into clean, structured text, saving you the hassle of manual formatting.

### Find the perfect words

Typeless lets you effortlessly express ideas, ensuring your message is easy to understand and your points come across clearly.

![Personalized style and tone](https://typeless-static.com/webpage/assets/homepage/Personalization-progress.webp?t=9)

### Personalized style and tone

Typeless adapts to your tone, phrasing, and writing habits, creating dictation that feels like you. Your personalization progress highlights how closely Typeless aligns with the way you communicate.

![Personal dictionary](https://typeless-static.com/webpage/assets/homepage/Personal-dictionary.webp?t=10)

### Personal dictionary

Typeless recognizes the names, terms, and expressions that matter to you. Whether added automatically or manually, your vocabulary stays accurate across every app and context.

![100+ languages supported](https://typeless-static.com/webpage/assets/homepage/100%2B-languages-supported.webp?t=11)

### 100+ languages supported

Speak in any language or mix them seamlessly, and Typeless automatically detects your language and transcribes your speech. It is simple and effortless.

![Different tones for each app](https://typeless-static.com/webpage/assets/homepage/Different-tones-for-each-app.webp?t=17)

### Different tones for each app

Typeless adapts tone and style based on the app you are using, from work emails to casual chats, so everything fits the context.

## Translate

### Translates as you speak

Typeless instantly translates your speech into your chosen language with phrasing that reads like a local would write it.

## Ask anything

### Speak to edit selected text

Select any text and speak your command. Instantly make it shorter, longer, change its tone, or even generate a creative response. It is your personal editor, ready anywhere you write.

### Speak to ask about selected text

Now you can interact with read-only text. Select a paragraph on any website or document and ask for a summary, an explanation, or a translation, all without breaking your flow.

### Quick answers & actions

Just ask for what you need. Typeless can check the latest info, help you brainstorm, search across sites and services, and open the right page with the details already in place, so you can stay in flow.

## Privateby design

On deviceCloud Sync

### Zero cloud data retention

Your voice dictations are processed securely with zero data retention.

### Never trained on your data

Your dictation data is never used for model training by us or any third party.

### On-device history storage

Your dictation history stays on your device. Only you can access it.

### No audio stored in the cloud
